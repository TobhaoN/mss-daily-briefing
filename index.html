Spyne announces data integration with Cox Automotive's Dealertrack DMS, benefiting dealer customers

Spyne announced on Oct. 22 that its Vini AI assistant now connects to Dealertrack DMS for mutual customers. Depending on the API scope a dealer enables, Vini can work with inventory, sales, customer and vehicle data, service repair orders and parts information, so it acts on the store's real operating data instead of running as a standalone layer. Spyne says it supports more than 3,000 dealerships.

What it means for us: Third-party AI vendors are choosing to build on Dealertrack, which is proof the DMS is open, not a wall. Pair it with the field's "zero integration fees" story against CDK and Reynolds, but confirm the fee terms for this integration with the Dealertrack team before you quote them.

Automotive Market

New-vehicle sales holds steady in Oct. as monthly payments reach new record

J.D. Power and GlobalData forecast October new-vehicle sales flat at a 15.6 million SAAR, with retail down 1.4% on a selling-day-adjusted basis. The average monthly payment is expected to hit an October record of $823 as loan rates reach 6.81%. Incentives are up 13.1% to $3,304 per vehicle, hybrids are climbing to 18.6% of retail sales, and 29.6% of trade-ins are expected to carry negative equity.

What it means for us: This is a payment month, not a price month. Get the payment and the trade payoff on the screen early with Deal Central and Dealertrack F&I, and use vAuto to stock and price the used hybrids buyers are shifting toward.

Talk track: "October's a payment month, with $823 on average, so let's put the payment in front of the customer in Deal Central before they have to ask."

Car shopping is changing: Is your dealership still discoverable

A piece bylined by Tessa Nadik draws on the Cox Automotive AI in Auto Retail Tracker: 63% of shoppers plan to use AI on their next vehicle purchase, but only 29% of dealers have started adapting to AI-powered search. Another 32% know they need to adjust but haven't started. It urges dealers to keep inventory, pricing and store details accurate and consistent so AI tools describe them correctly, and notes only 29% of buyers now know exactly what they want at the start, down from 37% in 2020.

What it means for us: AI visibility is a data-quality problem, which makes it a Dealer.com and DDC Advertising conversation. Clean inventory, pricing and store content on the dealer's site and Autotrader listings is what AI tools pull from. The tracker numbers date to August, but this is fresh Cox positioning sellers can use today.
