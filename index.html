Car buyers more frustrated with the purchase experience in September

CDK's Ease of Purchase score fell below 80% for the first time since December 2025, to 79%. Pain points spanned availability (only 45% bought off the lot), credit approval (down to 56%), paperwork, and time — a near-record 39% said the deal took longer than expected.

What it means for us: This is the data case for Dealertrack digital contracting and Deal Central — the friction CDK (our competitor) is measuring is exactly what a streamlined Cox deal workflow removes, especially the "40 minutes staring at papers" complaint.
