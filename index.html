CallRevu study: 60% of sales reps never ask for the appointment

CallRevu scored ~149,000 real sales conversations from 965,351 calls across 53 rooftops and found 60% of reps never asked to book an appointment — the single biggest driver of lost visits. Reps who did ask lifted set rates from 27% to 44%; pairing the ask with a trade-in question drove a 46% lift, and one store jumped from 13% to 56% using the same leads and reps.

What it means for us: This is a first-party-data win with zero incremental ad spend — a natural VinSolutions story. Call/CRM analytics that flag missed appointment-asks and trade-in questions turn existing leads into shown appointments, which is a far cheaper "media buy" than more clicks.
Talk track: "Before you raise your ad budget, let's score your inbound calls in VinSolutions — if 6 in 10 reps aren't asking for the appointment, that's 25 extra sold units a month hiding in the leads you already paid for."
