Automotive Market

U.S. to bar Polestar sales from model year 2027 over China software ties

The Commerce Department denied Geely-owned Polestar authorization to sell MY2027 vehicles under the Connected Vehicle Rule, which restricts vehicles containing Chinese or Russian software. Existing Polestar 3 and 4 stock can still be sold; the bigger story is the precedent for any brand with Chinese software content, including the China-built Lincoln Nautilus.

What it means for us: The Connected Vehicle Rule reshapes which VINs are legally sellable, which feeds directly into vAuto stocking and appraisal logic — dealers will ask whether affected inventory and residual forecasts are flagged before they buy at a Manheim lane.

Talk track: "If you're appraising or stocking any Geely-family or China-software VIN, vAuto's market data is where you want to catch the residual risk before it hits your lot — not after."
