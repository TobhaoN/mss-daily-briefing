Chinese EV makers are outpacing U.S. automakers in overseas investments

Chinese automakers announced roughly $101B in overseas EV and battery investments from 2019–2025 versus about $38B for U.S. companies, per Atlas Public Policy, with analysts warning BYD and peers are becoming the "new GMs and Fords" of the EV era. Saturated domestic demand, strong export appetite, and tariff-driven local factory builds are fueling the shift.
