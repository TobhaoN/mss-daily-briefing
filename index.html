Automotive Market

Mexico auto exports fall 12% as U.S. tariffs pressure production

Mexico's light-vehicle exports fell 12% in September to 277,369 units, the steepest drop since December 2025, and production fell 15%. Mexican-built vehicles face a 25% U.S. tariff while the USMCA review continues. GM, Ford and Nissan exports fell sharply, while Kia, BMW and Mazda grew. Mexico still supplies 16% of U.S. light vehicles.

What it means for us: Changes how dealers stock and price. Supply from Mexico-built models gets tighter and less predictable. vAuto Conquest and Stockwave help fill gaps with used units, and Provision keeps pricing honest when new supply wobbles.

Talk track: "Mexico just shipped 12% fewer vehicles, so if your new mix leans on Mexico-built models, let's use Stockwave and Conquest now to backfill before the gap shows up on your lot."

Dealership & Retail Tech

Why dealers are betting on F&I as front-end margins shrink

Ascent Dealer Services CEO Adam Marburger says thin front-end margins are pushing dealers to lean harder on F&I and fixed ops. Record loss ratios sank some lifetime programs, so stores are now building reserves. He says meeting the F&I manager early makes the menu conversation easier, and remote F&I can work with the right tech partner.

What it means for us: Changes how we sell Dealertrack F&I and Deal Central. When the front end shrinks, menu presentation and product penetration carry the store. Introduce products early in the online deal so F&I isn't a cold ambush at the end.

Talk track: "Front-end gross is getting squeezed, so let's put your products in front of the buyer early in Deal Central and let Dealertrack F&I close them, in store or remote."
