Honda commits $3B to new Ohio plant as hybrid production expands

Honda will build a fourth Ohio auto plant with capacity for 240,000 vehicles a year, starting production in 2029 and adding more than 2,000 jobs. It is also converting an Ohio transmission line to raise hybrid system output by 250%, pushing U.S. capacity to about 1.3 million units.

What it means for us: Honda and Acura stores will ask how to sell and service a growing hybrid mix. That's a natural Xtime service-lane and Dealer.com hybrid-inventory merchandising conversation.
