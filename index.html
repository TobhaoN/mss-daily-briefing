TOP STORY

Ford's new AI assistant rolls out to the mobile app — aiming at 8M owners

Ford began rolling out an AI assistant in the Ford and Lincoln apps that answers vehicle-specific questions — fuel needs for a trip, towing/cargo capacity, upcoming service — using the connected car's own telemetry. It plans an in-vehicle voice version in 2027 and is keeping it free for now. Ford says it deliberately went "app-first" to reach existing owners rather than waiting for new-vehicle software updates.

What it means for us: If the OEM owns the in-app service conversation, the dealer risks losing the fixed-ops touchpoint. This is the week to pitch Xtime and Dealer.com as the dealer-branded service and scheduling layer so the retention relationship stays with the store, not Ford's chatbot.

"Your agent didn't hallucinate — it exceeded its authority": the AI governance gap

This VentureBeat guest piece argues enterprises are solving AI safety but not AI authority — an agent can act correctly yet do something the business never sanctioned (e.g., issue an over-limit refund). What it means for us: As dealers turn on AI inside CRM and F&I, they'll ask "what can it actually do on its own?" Frame VinSolutions AI features around defined guardrails and human approval on money-moving actions — governance is a selling point, not a footnote.

VentureBeat (contributed) · Aug 10, 2026

Anthropic makes Claude Code's "auto mode" the default

Starting Aug 14, Anthropic will default Claude Code to auto mode for Pro, Max, and Team accounts — acting without step-by-step approval unless an action is "irreversible, destructive, or aimed outside your environment." Anthropic says testing showed auto mode caught 89% of harmful actions vs. 13.6% for habitual human review, and points to new prompt-injection screening and hard-deny rules.

What it means for us: The market is normalizing "agents act by default, humans handle exceptions" — the same model dealers will expect from AI in VinSolutions and Dealer.com. Use it to set expectations that autonomy plus enforced deny-rules is the direction, not a gimmick.
