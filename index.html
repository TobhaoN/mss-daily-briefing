Cox CPO study: 46% of shoppers are high-potential CPO buyers, but understanding is slipping

Cox Automotive's 2026 CPO study of 825 in-market shoppers found CPO appeal holding at 74% while new-vehicle appeal fell to 76% from 85% in 2023. More than half of shoppers are open to CPO, yet certified units are only about 12% of retail used sales. Familiarity fell to 81%, only 31% of new-vehicle shoppers open to CPO know exactly how it differs from used, and about two-thirds can't decode the acronym.

What it means for us: demand isn't the problem, explanation is. That puts vAuto (stock the right certified units), Autotrader (CPO badging and filters) and Dealer.com VDPs (spell out inspection and warranty) at the center of the dealer conversation this week.

Talk track: "Almost half your shoppers would pay more for certified, but most can't tell you what CPO means, so let's make your Autotrader listings and Dealer.com pages explain it for you."

LinkedIn angle: Two-thirds of shoppers don't know what CPO stands for. We built a whole premium category on an acronym nobody gets.

Public dealer groups now get 54% of gross profit from parts and service

Cox's chief economist says Q3 results from public dealer groups showed new GPU down 6% year over year to $2,703, while parts and service gross rose to $2.3 billion. Operating margin slipped to 3.8%. Cox calls the service lane a primary focus for years, given the oldest car parc on record.

What it means for us: this is the data behind the Xtime pitch. The service drive is the profit center and the "own auction block" for KBB ICO trade-ins, and Dealertrack DMS keeps fixed ops and sales on one record.

Presidio: more dealers want out as average store profit falls 10.9%

Presidio Group counts about 290 transactions covering 395 dealerships through September, with stores sold up 8%. Slow OEM approvals and store reputation are now moving price.

Dealership & Retail Tech

One month into California's CARS Act, a Toyota GM shares what changed

Toyota of Riverside rebuilt its CRM processes and automated messages so total price disclosures go out and get documented before pricing talks move forward. Returns under the three-day used-car rule are small, about five a month on 300 to 350 used sales. The bigger hit is contracts in transit, so the store now holds some weekend contracts until Tuesday.

What it means for us: California dealers will ask whether their CRM proves the disclosure went out. That's a VinSolutions templates and activity-log conversation, paired with Deal Central's printed disclosures.
