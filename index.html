Detroit automakers warn USMCA revamp could add billions in annual costs

What it means for us: Higher landed costs flow straight to transaction prices and days'-supply strategy — expect dealers to lean harder on vAuto for appraisal/pricing agility and on VinSolutions to work aged inventory before any price hikes land.
