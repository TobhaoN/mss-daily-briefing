BMW lets shoppers build a car and book a test drive inside ChatGPT

BMW of North America launched a U.S. shopping plugin in ChatGPT. Shoppers describe what they need in plain language, get model and trim picks from BMW's configurator data, compare against in-stock cars, pull lease pricing and book a test drive at a participating BMW center without leaving the chat. BMW calls it the first time an automaker has taken a shopper from open conversation to a booked dealer appointment inside an AI assistant. unconfirmed

Automotive Market

New-vehicle prices top $51,000 for the first time in October

Kelley Blue Book put the October average transaction price at $51,109, a record and up 2.1% from a year ago. Incentives ticked up to 6.8% of ATP but stay below long-term norms. Midsize SUVs are now the top-volume segment, and cheaper segments in the $28K to $38K range grew volume almost 10%.

What it means for us: Record prices with light incentives mean payment is the whole conversation. Dealers need vAuto Provision to price used units as the smart alternative, and KBB ICO to fund the down payment with the trade.

Dealership & Retail Tech

FTC reaches settlement with Long Island dealer group over pricing that didn't hold up

The FTC announced a proposed order against Stream Auto Outlet, saying the group advertised low online prices it didn't honor and buried required down payments, fees and add-ons behind a pop-up. The order would force ads to show the full price consumers pay, minus government charges. It still needs a judge's sign-off. The FTC sent warning letters to 97 dealer groups back in March.

What it means for us: This changes how we sell Deal Central and Dealer.com. Let's be real. Price transparency is now enforcement, not a nice idea. Showing the real number online and carrying it into the F&I office through Dealertrack is the compliance story every GM wants to hear this week.

Talk track: "The FTC just made an example of a dealer whose online price didn't match the deal, so let's set up Deal Central so the number your customer sees online is the number they sign."

JM&A says F&I carried dealers in Q3 as front-end gross slipped

JM&A Group's Q3 trends report shows F&I PVR and product penetration ahead of last year while front PVR fell. Product income made up 61.7% of F&I PVR. Average rates sat around 11.32% used and 8.07% new after the September Fed hike. The data covers 2,000+ JM&A dealers, not the whole market.

What it means for us: The back end is paying the bills. That's the opening for Dealertrack F&I and digital menus. Consistent product presentation, online and in store, protects the PVR that's keeping stores profitable.

Judge tosses Tekion's antitrust claims against CDK

A federal judge in Northern California granted CDK Global summary judgment and dismissed Tekion's monopoly claims. The court found no evidence CDK held monopoly power during the period in question. The full ruling sits behind Law360's paywall, so the details are thin for now.

What it means for us: Two of our biggest DMS rivals are still burning time and money fighting each other over data access. That's a gift for Dealertrack DMS. Zero integration fees and an open partner program look even better when the other guys are in court about who gets to plug in.

Cox Automotive Ecosystem

Dealertrack credit index hits its highest level since 2015

The Dealertrack Credit Availability Index rose for a sixth straight month to 107.1 in October. Approval rates reached 73.9% and subprime share climbed to 17.9%. Negative equity hit 58.7% and nearly a third of loans now run past 72 months. Cox flagged that the approval bump partly came from a near-prime lender leaving the market.

What it means for us: Credit is open but the deals are getting heavier. Underwater trades are now the norm. That's the case for Dealertrack's lender network to find the approval, and for KBB ICO and vAuto to value the trade honestly so the negative equity doesn't surprise anyone in F&I.
