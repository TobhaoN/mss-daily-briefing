Security advisory: Critical remote code execution vulnerabilities, update now

The PHP project shipped emergency releases on Oct. 21 to fix two critical flaws that can let an attacker run code on a web server without logging in. It says every PHP installation serving web requests is potentially at risk, and that updating is the only complete fix. Press reports say AI-assisted research helped surface the bugs, and some government and company sites went offline while operators patched.

Automotive Market

Negative equity hits 29.1% of trade-ins, rollover loans top $57,000

Edmunds data shows 29.1% of financed trade-ins toward new vehicles were underwater in Q3, up from 28.1% a year ago, with an average deficit of $7,107. Buyers rolling that debt into a new loan financed about $57,200 on average and pay roughly $950 a month, and more than 91% of them took terms of 72 months or longer.

What it means for us: Three in ten trade-ins come with a problem attached, so the payoff conversation has to move up front. Use KBB trade values and Deal Central to put equity and payment on the screen early, and Dealertrack F&I to structure the deal before the customer hits a surprise in the box.

Talk track: "Three in ten of your trade-ins are underwater, so let's show the payoff at the start of the deal in Deal Central, not as a surprise in F&I."

General Motors to add hybrid models as rising fuel prices increase demand

Mary Barra told analysts GM will add hybrids in multiple key segments without extra capital spending, a shift as fuel prices push buyers toward Toyota, Honda and Hyundai hybrids. GM hasn't named models or timing. Its U.S. sales fell to 671,000 in Q3 and share slipped to 16.1% from 16.8%.

What it means for us: Chevy, GMC and Buick stores will ask how to hold hybrid-curious shoppers until GM product shows up. Use Autotrader and KBB shopper data to see local hybrid demand, and vAuto to stock and price used hybrids now so those customers don't walk to the Toyota store.

VIP Auto Group replaces outside software with custom-built AI tools

VIP Auto Group CMO Ben Sporn says the group runs "friction audits" to find painful tasks, then builds its own AI tools to fix them. It has built more than a dozen, with the biggest returns coming from replacing third-party software, and says it saves about $1 million a year and 5,000 hours a month. A parts ticket workflow drafts replies for staff to review and tripled productivity without cutting jobs.

Competitor Watch

Lithia & Driveway (LAD) Reports Third Quarter Results

Lithia posted record Q3 revenue of $9.9 billion, with used retail gross per unit up 11% to about $1,970 and aftersales margin at 59.5%. Same-store new units fell 2.7% and F&I per unit dropped 4.7%. Its reported earnings were lifted by an unrealized gain on its stake in Pinewood Technologies, a DMS provider.

What it means for us: The biggest retailer in the country is making its money in used and service while new margins shrink, which is the vAuto and Xtime story in one quarter. And with an equity stake in Pinewood, Lithia has skin in a DMS that competes with Dealertrack. Know that before you walk into any Lithia rooftop.
