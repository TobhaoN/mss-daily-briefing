TOP STORY · Cox Automotive Ecosystem

Affordability challenges reshape automotive demand

Cox chief economist Jeremy Robb says demand hasn't vanished, it has shifted. Personal incomes are up about 48% since the pandemic while expenses are up 53%. The average new hybrid runs about $5,000 below the overall new average. vAuto Live Market View has the average used car on a lot just over $27,000, up 7%, with 8 to 10 year old units up just under 10%. Buyers 65 and older now make up about 27% of new-vehicle purchases.

What it means for us: Changes how we sell vAuto and Xtime. Older used inventory is where a lot of buyers are going. Dealers need Provision to price it, iRecon to recondition it fast, and Xtime service-lane acquisition to source it before it hits auction.

Automotive Market

U.S. EV sales rise 7.7% in Q3 as market rebounds

Kelley Blue Book estimates U.S. EV sales hit 266,630 in Q3, up 7.7% from Q2 and the best quarter in a year, though still down 38.6% from last year's incentive rush. EVs took about 6.5% share, and nearly 25% of new vehicles sold carried some form of electrification. Toyota and Lexus EV sales grew fivefold while Tesla fell 20% but still held more than half the market.

What it means for us: Changes how we sell vAuto and Dealer.com. The post-incentive dip is flattening, and a quarter of new sales are electrified. Dealers need EV and hybrid pricing dialed in and merchandising that explains range and cost to own.

Talk track: "KBB just showed EVs had their best quarter in a year, so let's make sure your Dealer.com listings and vAuto pricing are ready for the shoppers coming back."

Dealership & Retail Tech

Where AI helps and where dealers still need the human touch

Easton University founder Matt Easton shopped for a two-door Bronco and got mostly short texts with numbers and no next step. One store just sent a photo of a business card. The rep who won asked why he wanted the truck and sent an excited walkaround video. Easton also says a personal call from a service advisor buys more patience than any automated update.

What it means for us: Changes how we sell VinSolutions and Xtime. AI should handle speed and reminders so reps have time for the human part. Pitch VCA and video tools as the turbocharger, not the replacement.
