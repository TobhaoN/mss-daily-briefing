New-car loan rates hit about 10.5%, and markets now expect more Fed hikes

Cox's weekly summary puts new-car loan rates near 10.5% and used near 15%, with markets now pricing zero odds of no hike in January. Lenders are still saying yes: the Dealertrack Credit Availability Index is at its best level since 2015. But 58.7% of trade-ins on new loans carry negative equity.

What it means for us: payment is the deal now. Deal Central lets the desk show several payment paths at once, and KBB ICO equity numbers help offset negative equity before it kills the deal.

JD Power: the best borrowers are losing trust in their auto lenders

J.D. Power says satisfaction fell 8 points to 735 among financially healthy borrowers, who make up 47% of customers. Trust dropped 8 points to 737, and the new-customer experience fell 14 points. Audi Financial Services and Ford Credit ranked first in their segments.

What it means for us: the finance office sets the tone for the whole loan. Dealertrack F&I digital contracting and a clear Deal Central presentation set expectations up front, so good borrowers don't feel surprised later.
