Cox Automotive AI in Auto Retail Tracker: dealers and AI-powered shoppers are drifting apart

Cox Automotive's new quarterly Tracker (Q1–Q2 2026) found 82% of dealers now use AI, but the share who know they must adapt to AI-powered vehicle search yet haven't started rose from 26% to 32%. GM sets up $4.5B facility to guard against parts-supply shocks

In a regulatory filing, GM disclosed a $4.5 billion purchasing facility using a third party (Procura) to pre-buy critical parts and free up working capital, guarding against disruptions like disasters, cyberattacks or demand spikes. Banks including JPMorgan and Santander fund the program.

What it means for us: Fresh proof that supply resilience — including cyberattack risk — is top of mind for OEMs; a useful lead-in when dealers question inventory reliability and how vAuto helps them price against uneven stock.

Reuters · Aug 11, 2026

US new-vehicle inventory flat at 2.94M — 78-day supply

Inventory began August essentially flat at 2.94 million units (a 78-day supply), per Catalyst IQ. Hybrids stayed the fastest-turning powertrain at 68 days, while EVs climbed to a 90-day supply and ICE sat at 80. Only 21% of active inventory was priced under $35,000.

What it means for us: The EV-vs-hybrid days-supply split is a live vAuto conversation — dealers sitting on 90-day EVs need pricing-to-market discipline, while fast-turning hybrids justify more aggressive acquisition through Manheim.

Manheim Index: used-EV wholesale values are outperforming

The July Manheim Used Vehicle Value Index rose to 210 (up 1.3% year over year), but the EV sub-index jumped 10.5% while non-EVs edged up just 0.4%. Cox analysts tie firm used-EV demand to higher gas prices and the end of the new-EV federal tax credit.

What it means for us: Used EVs appreciating is a concrete vAuto and Manheim story — dealers who've been discounting off-lease EVs out of fear are mispricing appreciating stock, and we have the wholesale data to prove it.

Anthropic will watermark Claude-generated text to meet EU rules

Anthropic confirmed it will embed watermarks in text and files produced by its models to comply with the EU AI Act's Transparency Code. The mark is applied at the model level and is designed to persist through copy-paste and some editing; Google, Meta, Microsoft and OpenAI have made similar commitments.

Talk track: "As AI-content watermarking spreads, dealers leaning on generic AI copy will get flagged — Dealer.com content built for authenticity and local SEO is how you stay both compliant and credible."

What it means for us: Dealers generating marketing copy with AI (a top Tracker use case) will start asking about disclosure and authenticity — a timely reason to steer them toward Dealer.com/DDC managed content over anonymous AI output.
