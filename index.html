TOP STORY · Competitor Watch

Carvana tops 200K units in a quarter for first time, shares reporting updates

Carvana sold a record 217,385 retail units in Q3, up 39% year over year, on $8.04 billion in revenue, and CEO Ernie Garcia said it is growing about 40% a quarter in a flat industry and closing in on the top spot in used cars. It also flagged early success selling Stellantis new cars, added retail production at two more ADESA sites, and spent $152 million on advertising in the quarter. Garcia said clearer fee rules help Carvana, which charges no doc fees, and called doc fees a "presentation innovation."

AUTOMOTIVE MARKET

Ford raises 2026 profit outlook despite higher costs and truck production challenges

Ford posted $2.7 billion in Q3 core profit and raised the low end of its 2026 adjusted EBIT guidance to $10.5 billion, while lifting its commodity cost estimate to nearly $2.5 billion. A supplier issue halted F-150 output for almost a week last month, and Ford is now targeting 55 to 60 days of supply instead of its usual 80 to 100.

What it means for us: Ford dealers will be running lean on trucks into year end. That's a vAuto pricing and Stockwave sourcing conversation, because a store short on new F-150s needs to price and find used trucks fast.
