Manheim Index falls below last year as September depreciation runs hot

The Manheim Used Vehicle Value Index landed at 205.9 in September, down 0.6% year over year and 1.1% from August. Chief economist Jeremy Robb says depreciation was steeper than usual for the season, with record diesel prices pushing those units' days' supply up fast. EVs and compact cars held up while midsize cars, pickups and SUVs kept sliding, and the three-year-old MMR index dropped 1.7%.
