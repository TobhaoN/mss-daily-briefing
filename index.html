COX AUTOMOTIVE ECOSYSTEM

Manheim index holds 2% above last year in October as depreciation normalizes

The Manheim Used Vehicle Value Index finished October at 206.8, up 2% year over year and 0.5% from September on an adjusted basis. Unadjusted prices fell about in line with a typical October, used retail days’ supply sits just under 50, and EV values are up 6.6% year over year while light-duty trucks dropped 4.3% unadjusted as gas prices stay high.

What it means for us: Steady values and normal supply mean the edge heading into the holidays is acquisition and turn, not waiting for prices to move. Dealers heavy on trucks should be repricing now, which is a vAuto Provision and Manheim data conversation.

EchoPark launches its biggest brand campaign ever with Owen Wilson

Sonic’s used-only EchoPark brand kicked off its largest single-brand marketing push Sunday during NFL broadcasts, running across TV, streaming, social, display and billboards. Sonic says only about 15% of shoppers know the brand, and it’s pitching prices up to $3,000 below competitors plus a new app with photo-based vehicle search. Orlando opens in December as its 15th market.

What it means for us: A well-funded used competitor is about to show up in living rooms across 14 markets shouting about price. Dealers in those markets will feel it on used gross, and the counter is vAuto pricing discipline plus Autotrader and DDC merchandising that makes their value obvious.
