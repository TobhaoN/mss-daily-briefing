'We're f****d': One VW GM on what dealers are missing in the tech shortage

Jennifer Briggs, GM of Volkswagen of Marion and a former tech-school dean, says the technician shortage is dealers' problem to fix. Her playbook: get into local high school and community college programs, go straight to the instructors, and open OEM training portals to students. She's adding a second shift so apprentices can work alongside master techs, and conservatively projects it could add $1.8 million in service revenue in 2027. LaFontaine Automotive Group already runs an 18-month apprenticeship that pays mentors $15,000 when an apprentice graduates with six to eight certifications.

What it means for us: Cox announced a pilot on Oct. 19 that gives tech-school students free access to Xtime software and training. That's proof Cox is investing in the same pipeline dealers are being told to build. Pitch Xtime as the tool new techs learn on, and frame service absorption as the ROI story for Xtime.

Talk track: "Your next tech is in a classroom right now, and Cox's Xtime pilot puts our software in those schools, so they show up already knowing your service lane."

LinkedIn angle: A VW GM says the tech shortage is an execution problem, not a talent problem. Consistency beats talent. The stores that show up at the local school every semester win the next decade of service.

California New Car Dealers Association's lawsuit against Scout, Volkswagen proceeds after judge denies second motion to dismiss

A federal judge on Oct. 22 again denied Scout and Volkswagen's bid to dismiss the California dealer association's suit, so the unfair-competition claims move into discovery. The CNCDA argues a 2023 state law bars automakers from selling affiliated brands without franchised dealers, and says Scout went around dealers by taking $100 deposits directly online.

What it means for us: Dealers will ask whether OEM direct sales is coming for them. The strongest defense is a store that already sells the way Scout wants to: Deal Central and Dealer.com digital retailing, so shoppers start the deal online with the dealer, not the factory.

Talk track: "Scout wants to take your customers' deposits online. Deal Central lets you take them first."
