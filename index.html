Penske Automotive agrees to go private in a $16.3 billion deal

Penske Automotive Group agreed to be taken private by an entity owned by Penske Corporation and Mitsui at $212 a share in cash, about an 8.8% premium to Wednesday's close. Penske will own about 64% and Mitsui 36%, and the stock will leave the NYSE. Closing is expected in Q1 2027 pending shareholder and regulatory approvals. Roger Penske called it an important next chapter and pointed to new opportunities with Mitsui.

What it means for us: One of the biggest dealer groups just told the market it wants to think long term without quarterly pressure. Private groups invest in process and tech that pay off over years. Expect platform-level conversations, so lead with the full connected stack, Dealertrack DMS, VinSolutions and vAuto, not one-off point tools.

Talk track: "Penske just bet $16 billion that dealerships are a long game, so let's talk about the systems that make your store worth more five years from now, not just next month."

Ford opens Fathom preorder invites for its sub-$30K electric pickup

Ford is taking sign-ups for the Fathom, a midsize electric pickup starting at $28,350 before a $1,595 destination fee, with official preorders opening in early 2027. It's the first vehicle on Ford's new Universal EV Platform and seats five adults. Every Fathom is BlueCruise-capable and offers bidirectional power. Ford has not released an EPA range yet.

What it means for us: Changes how Ford dealers should sell VinSolutions right now. A sub-$30K truck will pull in shoppers who've been priced out. Capture them early in the CRM and nurture them until preorders open, before they forget which store they asked.

Talk track: "Ford is collecting names for a $28K truck right now, so let's build a Fathom campaign in VinSolutions and make sure those hand-raisers know your store first."

Carvana expands same-day delivery to Jacksonville

Carvana launched same-day vehicle delivery for select Jacksonville-area customers on Oct. 14. Sellers can also schedule same-day pickup after an online appraisal, which gives Carvana faster access to local trades. Same-day service now runs in select markets across more than 20 states, backed by Carvana's own logistics network and regional reconditioning centers.

Volvo expands its automated driving partnership with Nvidia

Volvo Cars is expanding its work with Nvidia on next-generation driver assistance and automated driving, using Nvidia's Hyperion sensor setup, computer architecture and driving software. The first launch will be supervised driving, with drivers still responsible. Volvo's head of software engineering said the tech should arrive before 2030 and won't be limited to pricier models.

What it means for us: More cars are becoming software products. Dealers will need service teams and Xtime workflows that handle software features and updates, plus Dealer.com merchandising that explains what the car can actually do.
