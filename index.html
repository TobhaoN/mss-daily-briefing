TOP STORY · Dealership & Retail Tech

3 ways dealers can protect themselves as AI speeds up hackers

Two critical PHP flaws nicknamed PHP2Shell were patched Oct. 21, and exploitation attempts climbed into the millions afterward, a risk for dealer websites, plugins and lead forms that often run on PHP through outside vendors. Experts CDG spoke with urge dealers to inventory what they run, ask website vendors for four specific answers (software and versions, open critical fixes, a named contact with a patch timeline, and written security assurances), and keep AI tools off live DMS and CRM data by giving agents their own limited, logged credentials. One source said that to his knowledge Dealertrack and RouteOne have not been identified as affected, but recommended asking both directly.

What it means for us: Dealers will ask whether their Dealer.com site and Dealertrack integrations are exposed. Don't guess. Route the question to Cox's official support and security channels and get them a written answer, and use the AI-access advice to frame how VinSolutions and Dealertrack DMS data should be connected to any third-party AI tool.

Talk track: "That's the right question to ask, and I'm not going to guess, so I'll get you a written answer from our team on your Dealer.com site and integrations this week, and in the meantime let's make a list of every vendor touching your website so you know who owns what."
