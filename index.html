Automotive Market

Q2 EV data: the decline is shrinking, and the winners are shuffling

Q2 saw 247,226 new EVs sold, down 20.5% year over year — but that's a milder drop than Q1's −27.3% and Q4's −36%, per Cox Automotive's EV Market Monitor. Toyota more than doubled its EV volume to crack the national top five while Tesla shed nearly 19,000 units, and hybrids are forecast up ~9% for 2026.

What it means for us: The "demand you see now is demand you can plan around" framing is a vAuto stocking conversation — help dealers align EV/hybrid inventory to real local mix instead of chasing a segment, and use the brand-level share shifts to justify data-driven acquisition.

California launches new state EV rebates after federal credits end

California signed legislation creating a state EV rebate program — up to $3,500 on qualifying new EVs and $1,750 on used models — to backfill the expired federal tax credit. The Air Resources Board is expected to name participating automakers next month; California remains the largest U.S. EV market at roughly 20% of new sales.

What it means for us: California dealers will ask which brands qualify and how to surface eligible units — that's a Dealer.com merchandising and VinSolutions campaign play to target first-time EV buyers the moment the participating-brand list drops.

Kelley Blue Book: June new-vehicle ATP holds at $49,758 as buyers trade down

AI & Technology

DeepMind's Hassabis proposes a FINRA-style standards body for frontier AI

Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis published a framework calling for an independent, industry-funded "standards body" — modeled on FINRA — to review frontier models before release, initially voluntarily and later as a deployment requirement. It would build on the ad-hoc U.S. government reviews of Anthropic's and OpenAI's latest models.

What it means for us: Formal AI governance is coming to the tools dealers buy — it's a trust talking point when selling AI-powered Cox products (VinSolutions, vAuto), reassuring dealers that "responsible AI" isn't just marketing when even DeepMind is asking for oversight.
