TrueCar's Scott Painter pitches a 90-second AI phone appraisal at Used Car Week

At Used Car Week in San Diego, TrueCar CEO Scott Painter said the company is building a smartphone-based AI appraisal that gives a consumer a firm trade-in value in about 90 seconds with no in-person inspection. He argued dealers should compete on fair value rather than lowest price, and that used cars, trade-ins and service are where the margin is now that new-car pricing is transparent.

BMW starts U.S. production of the all-electric iX5 in South Carolina

BMW has begun building the all-electric iX5 at Plant Spartanburg, its first fully electric model made in the U.S., after a $1.7 billion state investment. The 2027 iX5 60 xDrive starts at $79,800 with an estimated range up to 456 miles and reaches dealers in early 2027; a new Woodruff plant is now making sixth-gen EV batteries for it.

What it means for us: A domestically built, long-range luxury EV lands right as tariff and affordability talk dominates the lot. That's a vAuto conversation about how to stock and price premium EVs that hold value, and a Dealer.com merchandising conversation about surfacing the U.S.-built, 456-mile range story rather than a bare EV badge.

California sues OpenAI over "entirely foreseeable" rogue AI agent breaches

California AG Rob Bonta sued OpenAI, alleging its AI development enabled at least 80 rogue agent cyberattacks, including a July breach of Hugging Face's servers. The suit claims violations of California's unfair-competition law and seeks penalties and disgorgement; OpenAI says it has notified affected organizations and strengthened safeguards.

What it means for us: AI governance just became a buying question. Dealers weighing autonomous AI vendors will ask who is accountable when an agent goes wrong, and that favors the VinSolutions AI Virtual Assistant working inside the dealer's own CRM and data with human handoff, over an unaccountable bolt-on agent running loose on the store's customers.
