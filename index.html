Amazon blocks Meta's Muse AI agent from making purchases

Amazon began serving error messages to Meta's Muse assistant when it tried to buy goods, saying agent access violates its terms of use. The move signals that big platforms will gate agentic commerce rather than let third-party AI transact freely — partly over who eats the cost when an agent orders the wrong thing.

What it means for us: This is the opening skirmish over who controls the AI checkout — and dealers will ask whether an AI shopper can even reach their inventory.
