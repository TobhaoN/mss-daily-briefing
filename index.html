TOP STORY · Competitor Watch

What could happen if Chinese vehicles come to the U.S.

Dunne Insights CEO Michael Dunne told CBT Live that Chinese brands now hold about 20% of new car sales in the U.K. and about a third in Australia, up from near zero a few years ago. He says dealers with a Chinese franchise usually get healthy margins for about a year, then the brands push product hard and margins compress. He also says Chinese automakers talk privately about going direct within about five years. unconfirmed the go-direct claim is one analyst's account of private talk.

What it means for us: Dealers will ask "should I chase a Chinese franchise if it comes?" this year. Our answer is the store's value lives in its process and customer data, not one brand. VinSolutions, Xtime and Dealertrack DMS keep that value with the dealer whatever badge is on the building.

Talk track: "Whoever's badge shows up next, your customer list, your service drive and your processes are what make the store worth money, so let's make sure those live in systems you own."

LinkedIn angle: Year one margins look great. Then the product push starts. Let's be real. The franchise you sign matters less than the customer relationships you actually own.

Automotive Market

Bestselling car prices hit a new record, CDK reports

CDK's Affordability Tracker puts the average price of the 10 bestselling passenger vehicles at a record $36,418 in September, up 2.7% from a year ago. Four Toyota models drove most of the gain and no Toyota carries more than $156 in incentives. Truck prices slipped 0.6% while truck incentives jumped 32% to $5,084 per unit.

What it means for us: Changes how we sell vAuto and Deal Central. Cars are getting pricier while trucks need more cash on the hood. Price used against that new-car ceiling in Provision, and show real payments early in Deal Central so sticker shock doesn't kill the deal.

Talk track: "New bestsellers just hit a record price, so let's use vAuto to position your used units right under that number and catch the buyer who's priced out of new."

Hyundai offers 10% off the IONIQ 5 and IONIQ 9

Hyundai is offering 10% off, or up to $7,000, on its Georgia-built IONIQ 5 and IONIQ 9 as 2027 models arrive. The IONIQ 5 starts around $35,000 with a $3,500 discount, plus 0.9% APR for up to 60 months. Electrek reports the two EVs had the highest conquest rates in Hyundai's lineup last year.

What it means for us: Conquest-heavy EVs bring in new customers a dealer has never met. VinSolutions should catch them and Xtime should keep them coming back for service.

Your car may be recording your texts and selling your driving data

Lauren Fix's column cites Northeastern University and Consumer Reports testing of 21 connected vehicles and 30 companion apps. 28 of the 30 apps sent data to outside advertising or analytics firms, and seven sent personal details like a VIN paired with an email or location. She also points to the FTC's allegations that GM and OnStar shared driving data without proper consent.

What it means for us: Shoppers will get warier about who holds their data. Dealers using VinSolutions, Fullpath and Dealertrack need a clean answer on consent and how first-party data is used. Trust is part of the pitch now.
