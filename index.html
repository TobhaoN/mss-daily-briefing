Auto industry warns annual USMCA reviews threaten investment

Because the U.S. declined to renew USMCA for a full 16-year term on July 1, the pact now faces annual reviews for a decade. Automakers and suppliers warn the resulting uncertainty complicates long-horizon plant and battery investment decisions, even as existing tariffs keep raising costs.

What it means for us: tariff-driven cost uncertainty keeps new-vehicle affordability under pressure, pushing demand to used — reinforcing the vAuto/Manheim used-inventory playbook and the Dealertrack F&I tools dealers lean on when payments stretch.
