J.D. Power: new-vehicle quality jumps, but connectivity drags infotainment

The 2026 U.S. Initial Quality Study found the best year-over-year improvement since 1997 (175 PP100), with infotainment the lone category to worsen — driven by Android Auto/Apple CarPlay connectivity issues. Porsche ranked highest overall; Ford led mass-market brands.

What it means for us: Infotainment/connectivity is now the #1 early-ownership pain point, which lands squarely in fixed ops — an Xtime conversation about capturing those 90-day "tech glitch" visits as scheduled service rather than lost goodwill.

UVeye–CARFAX deal gives dealers deeper vehicle history

UVeye is integrating its drive-through inspection data (interior, exterior, underbody, tires) into CARFAX's vehicle history system, giving dealers a real-time-plus-history view to value used cars and estimate reconditioning. The data goes to dealers first, not yet consumer reports.

What it means for us: Richer condition data feeds straight into vAuto appraisals and reconditioning estimates — and UVeye is already a named Cox Navigator partner, so we can frame this as strengthening the same connected-inventory story rather than a competing data silo.

Talk track: "UVeye + CARFAX gives you the real condition picture — pair it with vAuto and you're pricing off reality, not guesswork, especially on service-lane acquisitions."
