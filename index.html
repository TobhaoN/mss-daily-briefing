Car buying got harder in September, CDK reports

CDK's Ease of Purchase score fell to 79%, the first reading under 80% since December. Credit applications took the biggest hit, with only 56% of buyers calling that step easy versus 70% in August. A near-record 39% said the deal took longer than expected, and fewer than half bought a car that was on the lot.

What it means for us: This is CDK's own survey saying the deal breaks at credit and paperwork. That is exactly the ground Deal Central and Dealertrack credit routing cover. Expect dealers to ask how to get financing handled before the customer walks in.

New-car loans reach record highs, even with rates flat at 7%

Edmunds says Q3 buyers financed a record $44,664 on average, with monthly payments hitting a record $787. Loans of 84 months or longer made up 25.5% of financed new-car deals, and 21.2% of buyers signed up for $1,000+ payments. APR held at 7.0%, so the extra cost comes from bigger loans stretched longer.

What it means for us: Payment is the whole conversation now. Dealertrack F&I menus and Deal Central payment-first desking let a store show term and payment tradeoffs fast, without the desk back-and-forth that kills deals.

Talk track: One in four new-car loans is now 84 months or longer. The store that shows payment options clearly and fast is the one that wins that buyer.
