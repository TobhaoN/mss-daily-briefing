Automotive Market

Trump doubles Canada auto tariff to 50%, effective January 1

After summer talks collapsed, the administration declared a 50% levy on vehicles, parts and trucks from Canada starting Jan. 1, up from 25%. Toyota and Honda — which build over 75% of Canada's 1.2M 2025 vehicles — plus GM's Silverado and Stellantis' Pacifica are most exposed; industry sources still hope for a deal before the deadline.

What it means for us: Landed-cost swings on Canada-built models will whipsaw new-car pricing and margins — expect dealers to lean harder on vAuto and Manheim data to reprice inventory and re-mix toward used and domestically built units as the Jan. 1 date nears.

Talk track: “If tariffs jump on your Canada-built lines in January, vAuto's live market data is how you re-price and re-stock before your competitor down the street does.”
