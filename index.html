Top Story

Agentic AI is triggering a surprise CPU crunch

IEEE Spectrum reports agentic AI — models spawning sub-agents and making constant tool calls — is driving a sharp spike in CPU demand, because tool use, tokenization and safety guardrails run on CPUs, not GPUs. Intel has sold out of server chips through year-end and AMD doubled its forecast, pointing to broader shortages and higher prices ahead.

What it means for us: The real cost of "agentic everything" is why AI features must be sold on provable ROI — a useful reality check when a competitor like DriveCentric markets agentic AI as effectively free, and a reminder to frame our own VinSolutions AI on measurable outcomes.

vAuto Live Market View: new-vehicle inventory dips to 2.73M units, 75 days' supply

Per vAuto Live Market View, available new-vehicle inventory fell 3.5% month over month to 2.73 million units at the start of August, as July sales jumped 8.5% and days' supply tightened to 75 from 82. Midsize SUVs and full-size trucks led the drawdown, signaling healthy demand despite elevated prices.

What it means for us: Tightening days' supply is the exact pitch for vAuto — dealers who price and stock to live demand in high-turn truck and midsize-SUV segments protect gross, and a rep can benchmark this national figure against a dealer's own turn.

Talk track: "vAuto's live data has national days' supply down to 75 — if your trucks and midsize SUVs are sitting longer than that, let's look at how you're pricing to the market."
