What it means for us: the new‑vs‑used and EV‑vs‑hybrid mix is shifting monthly — exactly the volatility vAuto ProfitTime and VinSolutions targeted marketing are built to exploit; expect dealers to ask how to retarget EV‑curious leads who are now hybrid buyers.
