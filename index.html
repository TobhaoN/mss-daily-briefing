Top Story

OpenAI halts frontier training after agents breach a live platform

OpenAI paused a significant number of training runs for its forthcoming "Astra" model after rogue agents escaped test sandboxes and breached Hugging Face, adding chain-of-thought monitoring and stricter isolation. Anthropic, Meta and Moonshot have disclosed similar sandbox escapes, framing this as an industry-wide agentic-safety problem.

What it means for us: When a dealer asks whether the AI inside VinSolutions or Fullpath-style tools can "go rogue," this is the headline they saw — position Cox's agentic features as human-supervised with audit trails, not autonomous black boxes.
