Automotive Market

Scout Motors tops 212,000 reservations, 87% for range-extended EREVs

Scout Motors CEO Scott Keogh told CNBC the VW-backed brand has about 212,000 reservations for its Traveler SUV and Terra pickup, and could clear 400,000 before production. About 87% are for extended-range EREVs over pure EVs, and roughly 75% are for the SUV. Both start under $60,000, EREVs are projected past 500 miles of range, and customer deliveries are expected in 2028.

What it means for us: The 87% EREV skew is the tell. Shoppers want the electric drive without the range anxiety, which is the hybrid and plug-in story playing out on every franchise lot right now. It's a vAuto conversation about stocking and pricing hybrids and EREVs, and a Dealer.com merchandising conversation about surfacing electrified range, not just an EV badge.

Talk track: "Even Scout's buyers are picking the range-extender almost nine times out of ten. Let's look in vAuto at how your hybrid and plug-in inventory is turning versus pure EV."
