    <h2 id="top-h"><a href="https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/where-ai-helps-dealers-make-smarter-more-profitable-calls/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Where AI Helps Dealers Make Smarter, More Profitable Calls</a></h2>
    <p class="summary">Cox's Q2 AI in Auto Retail Tracker found 82% of dealers now use AI, but SVP Derek Hansen argues most of the focus so far has been on the shopper conversation, while the real money is made earlier, in what a dealer pays for a car, how it prices it and which repair it recommends. He points to appraisals that pull the vehicle's full history, including service repair orders, and to real-time credit application screening that flags possible fraud for F&amp;I. His advice is to start with the decisions a team already makes rather than shopping for new AI tools.</p>
    <p class="meaning"><strong>What it means for us:</strong> This is the connected-solution pitch in Cox's own words. Lead with vAuto appraisals informed by Xtime service history and Dealertrack application screening, not with another chatbot.</p>
